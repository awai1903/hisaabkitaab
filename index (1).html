Pakistan ke roz ke calculators, free

Bijli bill, percentage, aggregate, zakat aur age. Koi signup nahi, sab kuch phone pe chalta hai.

Bijli bill calculator

Apne bill se units aur per unit rate dekh kar likho. Tariff aur taxes waqt ke saath badalte hain, isliye ye andaza hai.

Marks se percentage

Matric, inter ya university, kisi ke bhi marks ka percentage.

Merit aggregate calculator

Har university ka formula alag hota hai. Weights apni university ke mutabiq badal lo (default 10/40/50).

Zakat calculator

Zakat 2.5% hoti hai, agar total maal nisab se zyada ho. Nisab (silver ya gold ke hisaab se) aaj ke rate se khud likho, ya apne aalim se confirm karo.

Age calculator

Apni date of birth likho, saal, mahine aur din mil jayenge.
